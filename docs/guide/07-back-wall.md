# Stage 4 — Back wall

> **Goal:** the back wall — 97⅛" from deck to plate top, no openings — lies assembled flat on
> the deck: plates, thirteen identical studs, and the short double top plate that leaves the two
> back corners open for the side walls to lap.
> **Crew:** 1 person · **Time:** about two hours · **Weather:** dry deck

## Before you start

**Parts** (framing order, cut-list names):

| Qty | Part | Size | Cut length |
|---:|:---|:---|---:|
| 1 | back wall bottom plate | 2×4 | 16' 0" |
| 1 | back wall top plate | 2×4 | 16' 0" |
| 1 | back wall double top plate short | 2×4 | 15' 5" |
| 13 | back wall studs | 2×4 | 7' 8⅝" (92⅝" pre-cut) |

- Same stock as the front wall: 2×4 KD SPF #2, fasteners your own (not on either order), no
  sheathing.
- This wall stands on the deck's **back edge**, outer face flush with the deck edge.
- The thirteen studs are all the standard 92⅝" pre-cut length — no measuring, one saw setting.

## Steps

### 1. Mark both plates

Lay the bottom plate and top plate together on the deck, ends square. Hook the tape on the
**left end — the same end that is at your left facing the shed from the street** (on this wall
you are working from behind the shed, so that end is at your right as you look at the wall).
Mark the left edge of every stud on both plates:

| Tape reading from the left end | Stud |
|---:|:---|
| 0" | left-corner stud, flush with the plate end |
| 14½" | stud |
| 30½" | stud |
| 46½" | stud |
| 62½" | stud |
| 78½" | stud |
| 94½" | stud |
| 110½" | stud |
| 126½" | stud |
| 142½" | stud |
| 158½" | stud |
| 174½" | stud |
| 190½" | right-corner stud, flush with the plate end |

After the first two, the studs land 16" on centre.

### 2. Stand the thirteen studs

Set a 92⅝" pre-cut stud on every mark between the two plates, each one on the same face line,
and fasten both ends.

![Fig 7.1 — Back wall framing, elevation viewed from behind the shed, assembled flat (Stage 4).
Thirteen identical studs; the double top plate stops 3½" short of each end, leaving the corners
for the side walls' plates.](figures/fig-07-a.svg)

### 3. Nail on the gang-marked top plate

Lay the top plate you marked in step 1 on the stud ends, each mark aligned with its stud, and
fasten it through into every stud.

### 4. Add the short double top plate

Lay the 15' 5" double top plate on top, centred so it stops **3½" short of each end** of the
wall. Those 3½" corner blocks are deliberate: the two side walls' double top plates will lap
over this wall's corners and nail the back corners together, so the laps alternate around the
box — the front wall's full-width double plate ties the front corners, the side walls' plates
tie the back ones. If this plate ran the full 16' 0", the laps would stack on top of it instead
of interlocking.

## Before you move on

- [ ] Diagonals of the flat wall equal within ½" — checked with the tape.
- [ ] Thirteen studs, each 92⅝" — checked once with the tape; they are identical pre-cuts.
- [ ] Double top plate is 15' 5" and ends 3½" short of each wall end — checked with the tape.
- [ ] Every stud and both plates fastened; wall flat and square on the deck, ready for Stage 6.
