# Stage 5 — Side (rake) walls

> **Goal:** both side walls — the shed's rake walls, 97⅛" from deck to plate top — lie assembled
> flat on the deck. The two are the same rectangular build; only the right one carries a door
> opening.
> **Crew:** 1 person · **Time:** about half a day for both walls · **Weather:** dry deck

## Before you start

**Parts per wall** (framing order, cut-list names — the rectangular build is identical both
sides):

| Qty | Part | Size | Cut length |
|---:|:---|:---|---:|
| 1 | side wall bottom plate | 2×4 | 5' 5" |
| 1 | wall top plate | 2×4 | 5' 5" |
| 1 | wall double top plate | 2×4 | 5' 8½" |
| 5 | side wall studs | 2×4 | 7' 8⅝" (92⅝" pre-cut) |

**Extra parts for the RIGHT wall only** (its door opening):

| Qty | Part | Size | Cut length |
|---:|:---|:---|---:|
| 1 | right wall bottom plate short | 2×4 | 8½" |
| 1 | right wall bottom plate long | 2×4 | 1' 8½" |
| 2 | right wall jack studs | 2×4 | 6' 10½" |
| 3 | right wall headers | 2×4 | 3' 3" |
| 2 | right wall cripple studs | 2×4 | 5⅛" |

(On the right wall the two bottom-plate pieces replace the one 5' 5" plate.)

- Each wall is framed flat along its own deck edge — left wall on the left edge, right wall on
  the right edge, outer face flush with the edge. Both go up in Stage 6. The **front end** of
  each side wall is the street end.
- Same rules as before: 2×4 KD SPF #2, fasteners your own, no sheathing.

## Steps — the shared build (do it twice: left wall, then right)

### 1. Mark the plates

Lay the 5' 5" bottom plate and top plate together, ends square, and mark from the **front
(street) end** — on the left wall that is the end toward the street side of the deck. Left edge
of each stud:

| Tape reading from the front end | Stud |
|---:|:---|
| 0" | front-end stud, flush with the plate end |
| 15½" | stud |
| 31½" | stud |
| 47½" | stud |
| 63½" | back-end stud, flush with the plate end |

Five studs, 16" on centre, one at each end.

### 2. Set the five studs

Set the 92⅝" pre-cuts on their marks between the plates, same face line, and fasten both ends.

![Fig 8.1 — Left side wall framing, elevation viewed from inside the shed, assembled flat
(Stage 5). The rectangular wall you build on both sides; above it, dashed, the sloped rake plate
and stepped studs that arrive in Stage 7.](figures/fig-08-a.svg)

### 3. Nail on the top plate

Align the gang-marked top plate with the studs and fasten it through into every stud.

### 4. Add the double top plate — it overhangs the back end

Lay the 5' 8½" double top plate flush with the **front end** of the wall. It runs 3½" past the
back end on purpose: that overhang laps over the back wall's corner when the wall is raised in
Stage 6, tying
the back-left (or back-right) corner together. It also carries the sloped rake plate later, in
Stage 7.

## Right wall only — frame the door opening

Skip this block for the left wall. On the right wall, the 36" door opening interrupts the
build:

### 5. Split the bottom plate

Instead of one 5' 5" plate, use the two short pieces: the **8½" segment at the front end** and
the **20½" segment at the back end**. The 36" gap between them is the door opening — it has no
sill; the threshold is open down to the deck.

### 6. Set the studs and jacks around the opening

Stud left edges, from the front end: 0", 5½", 46", 55", 63½". Jack left edges: 7" and 44½" —
both 6' 10½" jacks bear on their plate segment and carry the head.

### 7. Set the header and the two cripples

Build the header exactly like the front-wall headers — three 3' 3" boards, two on edge at the
faces plus one flat cap — and seat it on the two jacks, bottom edge **84" up from the wall's
bottom edge (a 7' 0" head)**. Mark the two cripples on the top plate at 19" and 32½" from the
front end and set the two 5⅛" cripples between the header top and the top plate.

![Fig 8.2 — Right side wall framing, elevation viewed from inside the shed (front end at the
right), assembled flat (Stage 5). The same rectangle as the left wall, split bottom plate and
all, with the 3' 0" door opening framed between the jacks.](figures/fig-08-b.svg)

## Before you move on

- [ ] Diagonals of each flat wall equal within ½" — checked with the tape.
- [ ] Each wall: five studs, all 92⅝" — checked with the tape.
- [ ] Each double top plate is flush with the front end and overhangs the back end 3½" — checked
      with the tape.
- [ ] Right wall only: opening is 3' 0" wide between jack faces and the header underside is 84"
      up from the wall's bottom edge — checked with the tape.
- [ ] All four walls now lie flat on the deck, complete. Next page raises them.
