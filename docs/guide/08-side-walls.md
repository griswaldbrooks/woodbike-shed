# Stage 5 — Side (rake) walls

> **Goal:** both side walls — the shed's rake walls, 97⅛" from deck to plate top at their
> front end — lie assembled flat on the deck. The left wall is a plain flat wall; the right
> one carries the 5' 4" brewery door opening. Above the plate tops the gable follows the
> roof — that sloped build arrives with the rake plates in Stage 7.
> **Crew:** 1 person · **Time:** about half a day for both walls · **Weather:** dry deck

- Each wall is framed flat along its own deck edge — left wall on the left edge, right wall on
  the right edge, outer face flush with the edge. Both go up in Stage 6. The **front end** of
  each side wall is the street end.
- The double top plate no longer runs to the back corner: the roof drops below the plate stack
  before the back wall, so the flat plate ends mid-wall and the gable (Stage 7) carries on.
  The 3½" **corner block** at the back end of the double-top-plate line and the **corner post**
  under it carry the corner rafter's back seat — frame them as drawn.
- Same rules as before: 2×4 KD SPF #2, fasteners your own, no sheathing.

## Steps — left wall

### 1. Mark the plates

Lay the 6' 5" bottom plate and the top plate together, ends square, and mark from the **front
(street) end**. Left edge of each stud:

| Tape reading from the front end | Stud |
|---:|:---|
| 0" | front-end stud, flush with the plate end |
| 15½" | stud |
| 31½" | stud |
| 47½" | stud |
| 63½" | stud |

Five studs, 16" on centre. The back 12" of the plate carries no flat stud — the gable's mitered
studs land there in Stage 7.

### 2. Set the studs, nail the top plate

Set the five 92⅝" pre-cuts on their marks, same face line, and fasten both ends. Align the
top plate with the studs and fasten it through into every stud.

### 3. Add the double top plate and the back corner block

Lay the 5' ¾" double top plate flush with the **front end**; it stops where the roof drops
past it (about 4¾" short of the last stud). Nail the 3½" corner block in the double-top-plate
line at the **back end**, flush with the back end of the bottom plate, and set the 7' 4⅛"
corner post under it on the bottom plate. The block and post carry the corner rafter later.

## Right wall — frame the door opening

### 4. Split the bottom plate

Instead of one long plate, use the two 6½" segments: one at the **front end**, one at the
**back end**. The 5' 4" gap between them is the door opening — no sill; the threshold is open
down to the deck.

### 5. Set the studs and jacks around the opening

From the front end: the 92⅝" corner stud at 0", flush with the plate end; jack left edges at
**5"** and **70½"** — both 6' 10½" jacks bear on their plate segment and carry the head. That
leaves a **6½" end stud** between the opening and each corner — the flagged minimum of the
door plan; keep it.

### 6. Set the header and the cripples

Build the header exactly like the front-wall headers — three 5' 7" boards, two on edge at the
faces plus one flat cap — and seat it on the two jacks, bottom edge **84" up from the wall's
bottom edge (a 7' 0" head)**. Mark the three cripples on the top plate at 22½", 38½" and 54½"
from the front end and set the three 5⅛" cripples between the header top and the top plate.
The two mitered studs that close the gap between the header's back end and the gable arrive
with the rake plate in Stage 7.

### 7. Back corner, same as the left wall

Nail the 3½" corner block in the double-top-plate line at the back end and set the 7' 4⅛"
corner post under it.
