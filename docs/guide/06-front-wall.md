# Stage 3 — Front wall (tall, barn opening)

> **Goal:** the tall front wall — 10' 3" from deck to plate top — lies assembled flat on the
> deck, with the one 8 ft barn opening framed: plates, kings, jacks, header and cripples all
> nailed off; east of the opening the wall is solid.
> **Crew:** 1 person (2 makes layout easier) · **Time:** about half a day · **Weather:** dry deck

## Before you start

**Parts** (framing order, cut-list names):

| Qty | Part | Size | Cut length |
|---:|:---|:---|---:|
| 1 | front wall bottom plate | 2×4 | 16' 0" |
| 1 | front wall top plate | 2×4 | 16' 0" |
| 1 | front wall double top plate | 2×4 | 16' 0" |
| 8 | front wall king studs | 2×4 | 9' 10½" |
| 2 | front wall jack studs | 2×4 | 6' 10½" |
| 3 | front wall headers | 2×4 | 8' 3" |
| 5 | front wall cripple studs | 2×4 | 2' 7" |

- Deck is done (Stage 2). This wall is framed flat along the deck's **front edge — the street
  side** — bottom plate outer face flush with the deck edge. It goes up in Stage 6.
- All wall lumber is 2×4 KD SPF #2.
- The barn door's west jamb is the **corner**: the header's west end bears on the built-up
  corner post (corner king + corner jack), not on a jack-and-king pair like the east jamb.
  That is the flagged corner-bearing header of the door plan — frame it as drawn.
- **Fasteners are not modeled and not on either lumber order** — supply your own framing
  fasteners. No wall sheathing either: the lap siding (Stage 10) is the wall surface, applied
  straight over this framing.
- Tools: tape, pencil, speed square, saw, hammer or driver.

## Steps

### 1. Mark the bottom plate

Lay the 16' 0" bottom plate flat on the deck, square with the deck's front edge, outer face flush
with the edge. Hook your tape on the **left end — the end at your left as you face the shed from
the street** — and mark every position in the table. Each mark is the **left edge** of that
member; the member is 1½" wide, running to the right of its mark.

| Tape reading from the left end | Member |
|---:|:---|
| 0" | left-corner king stud — sits on the plate |
| 2" | corner jack stud — the corner post's front-face ply, sits on the plate |
| 99½" | barn opening jack stud — sits on the plate |
| 101" | king stud |
| 117" | king stud |
| 133" | king stud |
| 149" | king stud |
| 165" | king stud |
| 181" | king stud |
| 190½" | right-corner king stud — sits on the plate |

<!-- model trace: tape reading = model x + 3.5 -->

### 2. Set the jacks and kings

Set the studs on their marks, all on the same face line of the plate:

- The two 6' 10½" jacks (2" and 99½") on top of the plate, bottoms at the plate.
- The eight 9' 10½" kings on top of the plate — including both corner studs, flush with the
  plate ends.

Fasten each stud to the plate. The corner king and the corner jack together are the built-up
corner post the header's west end bears on.

### 3. Build the header on its bearings

The header is **three 2×4s cut to the same length**: two standing on edge, one flush with each
wall face, with the third lying flat across the top of both, capping them. Assembled, the header
is 5" tall and the full 3½" wall depth. Build it from the three 8' 3" pieces. Set it spanning the
opening on the corner post at the west and the east jack, bottom edge **84" up from the wall's
bottom edge — a 7' 0" head over the deck** — flush with the wall faces, and fasten it to the
jack and to the corner post.

### 4. Add the cripples over the header

Mark the cripple left edges on the **top plate**, same tape readings from the left end: 18",
34", 50", 66" and 82". Set the five 2' 7" cripples between the header top and the top-plate
marks, and fasten both ends.

### 5. Nail on the top plate, then the double top plate

Lay the top plate over the stud ends, aligned with the bottom plate, and fasten it through into
every stud. Then lay the double top plate on top of it, full width, ends flush. The wall will be
10' 3" tall once it goes up. **Do not raise it yet** — all four walls are raised together, in
order, in Stage 6.

## Before you move on

- [ ] Diagonals of the flat wall equal within ½" — checked with the tape.
- [ ] The opening is 8' 0" wide between the corner post's inside face and the east jack face —
      checked with the tape.
- [ ] The header underside sits 84" up from the wall's bottom edge — checked with the tape.
- [ ] Every stud, jack, cripple and the header is fastened; the wall lies flat and square on
      the deck, ready to be raised in Stage 6.
