# Order — framing lumber + foundation

> **Goal:** one printed sheet that buys every framing and foundation item the shed needs, in one trip if your cuts go well.

This is the framing order. The finish lumber (siding, trim, doors, hardware) is a **different
order on a different page** — [03-order-finish.md](03-order-finish.md). Never merge the two.

**Yard: Hingham Lumber.** Kiln-dried framing species is SPF #2. The yard stocks 16 ft 2×4,
16 ft 4×4, and true 20 ft 2×6 — the order below assumes those stocks. Pressure-treated
ground-contact species is whatever the yard carries; that choice is not yours.

> ⚠️ **WARNING — exact quantities, zero overage** · a miscut is not covered by the order ·
> plan a follow-up run instead of padding the list. The stock packing below already includes
> a ⅛″ saw kerf per cut; it does not include mistakes.

## Foundation materials (not on either lumber order)

Per the captain's 2026-08-24 decision: the skids bear directly on one compacted gravel pad
across the whole 16′ × 7′ footprint — no pedestals, no paver pads, no fasteners.

| ☐ | Item | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | ¾″ angular crushed stone | about 3–4 yd³ | the whole pad, 8–12″ deep as dug, placed in 3″ lifts and tamped |
| ☐ | landscape fabric | one pad's worth | lines the whole excavation, lapped up the sides |

The pad is screeded flat and level to string lines, and the skids bed directly on it — no
shims anywhere. Decision record and source: [r02-sources.md](r02-sources.md).

The foundation is the one part of this build that is drawn, not modeled — see
[r02-sources.md](r02-sources.md) for where the drawings came from.
