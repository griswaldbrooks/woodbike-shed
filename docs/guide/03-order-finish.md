# Order — finish lumber + hardware

> **Goal:** one printed sheet that buys every siding, trim, door, and hardware item — without touching the framing order.

> **THIS IS A SEPARATE ORDER.** The finish lumber is modeled for real but ordered on a
> completely separate list from the framing lumber. Never merge the two orders, never buy
> finish stock "while you're there" on the framing run. Two lists, two trips, two invoices.

**Yard: Hingham Lumber.** Same yard as the framing order.

> ⚠️ **WARNING — exact quantities, zero overage** · a miscut is not covered by the order ·
> plan a follow-up run instead of padding the list, same as the framing order.

## Primed versus kiln-dried — read before substituting

- **PRIMED** rows (all siding, skirt, corner boards, frieze, door casings) arrive with a
  factory primer coat. They are the weather skin of the shed. They still need a finish coat
  of paint on site — paint and caulk are not on any order.
- **KD** rows (the 1×4 and 1×6 door stock) are bare kiln-dried boards for the
  board-and-batten leaves. They arrive unprimed: prime and paint them yourself before or
  after assembly.
- Do not swap one for the other at the yard. Primed stock is a different product line from
  framing-grade KD; and the door leaves are deliberately bare KD, not primed.

Hardware has no geometry in the model — hinges and latches exist only as the line items
above.
