# Stage 12 — Doors

> **Goal:** four board-and-batten leaves built and hung over the three openings, closing up the shed.
> **Crew:** 1 person, 2 for the double leaves · **Time:** about one day · **Weather:** dry

## Before you start

The trim is on and every opening is cased (Stage 11). The doors are the last layer of the finish:
each leaf hangs **on the casing face**, one layer proud of the trim, and its own frame rides one
layer proud again. Door stock is KD 1×6 and 1×4 from the separate finish order; hinges and
latches are line items on the same order — fasteners for everything else are not modeled and not
on either order.

Four leaves over three openings: one single on the front-wall 36″ opening, a centre double over
the front-wall 72″ opening, and one single on the right-wall 36″ opening. The two singles are
identical.

Tick off the parts (cut-list names, see [R01 cut list](r01-cut-list.md)):

| Qty | Part · stock | Cut length | Use |
|---:|:---|:---|:---|
| 30 | finish door planks · 1×6 KD | 6′ 10¾″ | vertical planks, ripped per leaf below |
| 8 | finish door rails · 1×4 KD | 6′ 10¾″ | the two full-height outside stiles per leaf — the cut list files stiles and rails under the one name |
| 4 | finish door rails · 1×4 KD | 2′ 8″ | top and bottom rails, the two single leaves |
| 4 | finish door rails · 1×4 KD | 2′ 6⅜″ | top and bottom rails, each double leaf |
| 8 | strap hinge 12″ black | hardware | 2 per leaf |
| 3 | gate latch + hasp black | hardware | 1 per opening |

Tools: tape, square, circular saw or mitre saw, table saw or ripping guide for the plank rips,
drill/driver, level, props or a helper.

## Steps

### 1. Rip the planks

Each leaf's planks are ripped from the 1×6s so they fill the leaf width exactly, edge to edge:

- **Single leaves (2):** 8 planks each, ripped to 4⅞″.
- **Double leaves (2):** 7 planks each, ripped equal — about 5 11/32″ apiece — so the seven
  together make the leaf width.

All planks stay 6′ 10¾″ long. Keep the ripped edges straight; they are the face of the door.

### 2. Build a single leaf

Stand the eight planks edge to edge, 39″ across — that is the 36″ clear opening plus the 1½″ the
leaf will lap onto each jamb casing. Build the 1×4 perimeter frame on the **outer face** of the
planks: the two 6′ 10¾″ stiles full height at the edges, the two 2′ 8″ rails across the top and
bottom between them. Screw the frame to the planks. The leaf is 82¾″ tall.

### 3. Build the double leaves

Build the pair for the 72″ opening the same way, mirrored: each leaf 37⅜″ wide with 7 planks,
the two 2′ 6⅜″ rails top and bottom, stiles full height on the outer face. Hung, the pair meets
with a ¼″ gap at the centre.

### 4. Hang the single leaves

Set each single leaf on its opening with its **bottom edge 1″ above the deck** and its top edge
¼″ below the 84″ head line, lapping 1½″ onto each jamb casing. Mark the hinge positions on the
casing at the top and bottom rail heights, then fix the two 12″ strap hinges: strap along the
leaf's rail, hinge end screwed to the casing face. The leaf swings on the casing — that is by
design, and it is why the casing is solid 1×6 over the studs.

> ⚠️ **WARNING** — pinch and drop hazard · a leaf is awkward one-handed · support it on a prop or
> to a helper while you fix the hinges, and keep fingers out of the hinge-side gap as it swings

### 5. Hang the double leaves

Hang the pair on the 72″ opening the same way, one leaf per side, hinged to the outer jamb
casings. Leave the ¼″ meeting gap at the centre and check both bottoms sit 1″ above the deck.
Fit the latch at the centre meeting stile.

### 6. Read the jamb build-up

Before you call the doors done, look at what a closed leaf stacks onto — this is the build-up the
whole finish was working toward, and the check for every layer you hung. At any door jamb the
finish steps outward in **four ¾″ layers**:

1. **1×8 lap siding** on the wall face,
2. **1×6 jamb casing** on the siding face,
3. **door planks** on the casing face,
4. the **1×4 leaf frame** outboard.

That is **3″ proud of the wall face** in total. The siding and the casing both stop dead at the
clear opening edge; the closed leaf laps onto each jamb casing by 1½″.

its 1½″ laps, and latch without lifting.

## Before you move on

- [ ] Every leaf's bottom edge 1″ above the deck, top edge ¼″ below the head — tape
- [ ] Every leaf laps its jamb casings 1½″ on both sides — tape
- [ ] The double leaves meet with a ¼″ centre gap — tape
- [ ] All hinges fixed at rail heights, straps on the leaf, screws in the casing face — look and try the swing
- [ ] Each opening's jamb reads four ¾″ layers out to 3″ proud — sighted across the jamb against Fig 15.3
- [ ] Every door swings freely and latches without lifting — by hand
